What BingoFit Is

BingoFit is a fitness bingo game for iPhone and Android. You pick a 5x5 board, and each tile is an activity goal such as steps, distance, active minutes, or a recorded workout. Your real activity fills the tiles, and completing the pattern wins the board. You can play solo or challenge friends on a shared board.

How It Uses Your Activity Data

BingoFit reads activity data from the health service you choose to connect on your phone, such as Apple Health or Health Connect. Access is read-only: BingoFit never writes to your health data. It uses daily totals of steps, distance, active minutes and exercise minutes to mark tiles complete and to show your stats and rankings. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
