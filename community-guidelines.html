BingoFit Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BingoFit is a place to get moving with friends. These guidelines keep it friendly and fair for everyone.

Be a good teammate

BingoFit is for encouraging each other to move. Keep usernames, photos, and board names friendly. No harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content.

Be yourself

Use a username and photo that represent you. Don't impersonate other people, brands, or BingoFit staff.

Play fair

Stats come from your connected health provider. Don't fabricate activity, use modified apps, or exploit bugs to win challenges or earn rewards.

Respect privacy

Only invite people who want to play. Don't share anyone else's personal information.

Reporting and blocking

Report a profile from its page or block a user to stop all contact instantly. We review reports within 24 hours. Repeated or serious violations lead to warnings, removed content, or account closure.

Age

BingoFit is not intended for children under 13.
