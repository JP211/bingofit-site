What BingoFit Is

BingoFit is a fitness bingo game. You pick a 5x5 board, and each tile is an activity goal such as steps, distance, active minutes, or a recorded workout. Your real activity from Apple Health or Health Connect fills the tiles, and completing the pattern wins the board. You can play solo or challenge friends on a shared board.

Common Questions

How do I connect Apple Health or Health Connect?

Open Settings, then Device, and tap Connect for your provider. The screen explains each kind of data before your phone asks for permission. New accounts are also offered this step right after profile setup. On Android you may need to install or update the Health Connect app first.

Why do my steps look wrong?

Open Settings, then Device, and tap Sync Now. Also keep in mind that activity from before a board started never counts toward that board, and that BingoFit reads from one provider at a time, so make sure the provider you connected is the one your phone or watch writes to.

How do subscriptions work, and how do I cancel?

BingoFit is free to play. BingoFit Premium is an optional monthly subscription that adds capacity and extras; it never changes scoring. It renews automatically until you cancel. To cancel, use your App Store account settings on iPhone or your Google Play subscriptions on Android. Deleting the app or your BingoFit account does not cancel a subscription, and the app itself cannot cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings, then BingoFit Premium, then tap Restore. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy.

What are rewarded ads?

They are optional. You are never forced to watch an ad. You can watch up to 4 a day, and each one gives you either a booster or a streak save, which you choose before the video plays.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, then Delete Account. If you cannot open the app, use the steps on the delete account page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I download my data?

Open Settings, then Download My Data. You get a machine-readable copy of your account data.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap the menu at the top right. Reports are reviewed within 24 to 72 hours depending on severity. See the Community Guidelines.
