BingoFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how BingoFit handles personal, fitness, and health data.

What BingoFit Collects

BingoFit collects the account and profile information needed to run the app, including your email address, username, avatar, friends, challenges, game progress, badges, boosters, and notification preferences.

Fitness And Health Data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Health Connect, or Google Health, BingoFit reads fitness data that supports your game stats. This may include steps or wheelchair pushes (based on your private activity-tracking setting), distance, active minutes, and recorded exercise (workout sessions). BingoFit uses this data only to update your stats, challenges, boards, badges, and related fitness progress in the app.

Health Connect Permissions

On Android, BingoFit requests read access to Health Connect steps or wheelchair pushes (based on your setting), distance, and exercise sessions. Exercise sessions update recorded exercise and, until the app can read active minutes directly from Health Connect, active minutes as well. BingoFit does not request heart rate, exercise routes, location, or Health Connect write access. You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time from Android settings or from the Health Connect app.

How Data Is Used

BingoFit uses your data to authenticate your account, display your profile, manage friendships and challenges, calculate game progress, sync fitness stats, send requested notifications, prevent abuse, and maintain app reliability.

Sharing

BingoFit does not sell your personal or health data. Your profile, game progress, and challenge information may be visible to friends or challenge participants as part of the app experience. Service providers such as authentication, database, storage, notification, and hosting providers process data only to help operate BingoFit.

Storage And Security

BingoFit stores app data using managed cloud services and may store limited cached fitness data on your device so the app can display recent stats. We use reasonable safeguards for the size and nature of the app, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention And Account Deletion

We keep account, fitness-derived, social, and game data while your account is active and it is needed to provide BingoFit. You can permanently delete your account from Settings. Deletion immediately removes your live sign-in, profile, uploaded avatar, fitness-derived data, boards and progress, friendships, notifications, device tokens, and challenges you created; shared challenges are updated so they no longer identify you. Service-provider operational logs and queues are deleted or de-identified within 30 days, and encrypted disaster-recovery backups expire within 90 days. We may keep minimized security or abuse-prevention records for up to 12 months, or records subject to a legal hold only as long as required. Data that has been irreversibly de-identified and can no longer reasonably identify you may be retained for aggregate reporting. Deleted backup data is not returned to live systems and will be deleted again if a disaster-recovery restore is required.

Your Choices

You can disconnect fitness services from the device connection screen. You can revoke Health Connect access in Android settings, Apple Health access in iOS settings, and Google Health access from your Google Account third-party access settings. You can download a copy of your data from Settings (Download My Data), sign out at any time, or permanently delete your account and associated data from Settings.

Access, Correction, And Export

You can see and change your username, profile picture, email address, and password from Settings. Download My Data in Settings gives you a complete machine-readable copy of your account data at any time, up to three times per hour. If something cannot be corrected in the app, contact us using the support address below and we will respond within 30 days. We verify requests by asking you to write from the email address on the account.

Service Providers

BingoFit runs on Supabase (authentication, database, file storage, and server functions, hosted in the United States), uses Expo push services to deliver notifications to your device, and uses Apple and Google sign-in services when you choose them. Profile pictures are checked by an automated moderation service (OpenAI) that receives only the image, never your name or account. Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat together with the App Store or Google Play; RevenueCat receives your anonymous account id and purchase status, never your health data. If you choose to watch a rewarded video (optional for everyone, never forced), it is served by Google AdMob; AdMob receives device advertising identifiers and ad interaction data (and, on iOS, only what you allow under App Tracking Transparency), never your health or activity data. These providers process data only to run BingoFit and are bound by their own privacy and security commitments. No data is sold, and no advertising network receives your health or activity data.

Regional Rights And International Transfers

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can exercise these rights through the app features described above or by contacting us. Your data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use BingoFit from another region, your data is transferred to the United States to provide the service.

Children

BingoFit is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, use the support contact listed on the Google Play Store page to request deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the support contact listed on the BingoFit Google Play Store page.
