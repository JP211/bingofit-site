BingoFit Your Data

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Your BingoFit data belongs to you. You can get a copy of it, correct it, or delete it at any time, free of charge.

Get a copy in the app (fastest)

Open Settings and tap Download My Data. You receive one file with a complete, machine-readable copy (JSON) of your account data, which you can save or share from your phone. You can do this up to three times per hour.

What the copy contains

Your profile; the challenges you created or joined, with your boards, tile progress, results and rewards; your monthly boards and cards; streaks and badges; booster history; friendships, invites you sent, and the list of users you blocked; reports you filed; notifications you received; and the daily activity totals BingoFit recorded for your boards. BingoFit stores daily totals only, never raw health samples.

Get a copy before you delete your account

Deleting your account removes your data from our systems immediately, so we cannot send a copy afterward. If you want to keep your data, use Download My Data first, then delete your account. The steps for deleting are on the delete account page.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@bingofit.fitness from the email address on your BingoFit account, with the subject "Request my data". We verify that the account is yours by replying to that address, then send your copy within 30 days. If your account has already been deleted, we will confirm that and tell you whether any record was kept for security or legal reasons.

Correct your data

You can change your username, profile picture, email address and password in Settings. If something cannot be corrected in the app, email us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.
