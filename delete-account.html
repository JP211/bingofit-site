BingoFit Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 5, 2026

You can delete your BingoFit account and all data tied to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm. Your profile, challenges, progress, friendships, notifications, and device tokens are removed immediately; encrypted backups expire within 90 days.

If you cannot open the app

Email the support address shown in the app Settings from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify ownership by replying to that address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is kept

Only records we must keep for security or legal reasons (for example, a moderation report about abuse), and only for as long as required. Health and fitness values are never kept after deletion.

Linked sign-in

If you signed in with Apple or Google, the app revokes that link during deletion. You can also remove BingoFit from your Apple ID or Google account settings.
